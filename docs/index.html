Omarchy Music

One player for YouTube Music, Tidal, Apple Music and Spotify, plus your own files. Everything plays in lossless FLAC from Tidal wherever Tidal has it.

Download

  • Windows

    Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. It installs for your account only, so no administrator rights are needed.

    Download for Windows (installer, .exe)

    The installer isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

  • Android

    Android 10 or newer, with a player in the notification shade and on the lock screen. It can also act as a remote for the desktop app.

    Download for Android (.apk)

    Open the file on your phone. Android asks you to allow installs from your browser or file manager the first time.

  • Linux

    Made for Omarchy (Arch with Hyprland), and it runs on any desktop with GTK 4.

    See the Linux install steps

Older versions and release notes are on the releases page.

Install on Linux

On Arch or Omarchy, run these in a terminal:

sudo pacman -S --needed mpv ffmpeg python-gobject gtk4 libadwaita
git clone https://github.com/Varixxl/omarchy-music.git ~/Projects/omarchy-music
~/Projects/omarchy-music/install.sh

Then start it with omarchy-music, or bind a key to it. Running it again shows or hides the window.

What it does

  • Four services, one player. Each song is matched on Tidal and streamed in FLAC, hi-res where Tidal has it.
  • Your own music. Files in your music folders play next to everything else.
  • Search where you want. Tidal, YouTube Music or your own songs, with artists and albums in the results.
  • A DJ, like Spotify's. Sets of the songs you love, ones you haven't heard in a while and new ones from Tidal and YouTube Music. Ask it for a mood, a decade, a genre or an artist.
  • Bring your playlists. Import .m3u8 playlists from other players.
  • Downloads only when you ask. Save a song, an album or a whole playlist as FLAC.
  • Sort your music by title, artist, album or date added.
  • Updates itself. Each app tells you when a new version is out and installs it when you pick Update in its menu.
  • Lyrics. From Genius, or lines timed to the music.
  • EQ. A 10-band equalizer with headphone correction curves.
  • Last.fm. Plays are scrobbled, and Wrapped looks back on any year. On Android, Wrapped can add your Spotify history too.
  • Phone remote. The Android app can control the desktop app on the same network, and play the phone's own songs there.

Before you start

Do I need a Tidal subscription?

For lossless, yes: FLAC comes from Tidal and needs a Tidal plan. Songs Tidal has no FLAC of, or everything without Tidal, stream in lower quality from Tidal's AAC or YouTube Music. YouTube Music, Apple Music and Spotify are used to browse your library and playlists.

How do I sign in to YouTube Music or Apple Music on Windows?

Sign in at music.youtube.com and music.apple.com in Firefox, then reopen the accounts menu (☰) in the app. The app reads those sign-ins from Firefox. Chrome and Edge lock their cookies so that other apps can't read them.

Where does it keep my data?

Downloads go to Music\Omarchy Music in your user folder. On Windows, settings and logins are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\omarchy-music. On Linux they are in ~/.local/state. Nothing leaves your computer except requests to the music services you connect.

Is it free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT license. The source code is on GitHub.
